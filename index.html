Planned like with an assistant. Private like on paper.

Kaboome combines automatic time blocking with a calendar whose contents can only be read on your own devices.

⏱

Automatic time blocking

Effort, priority and deadline are enough. Kaboome spreads the work over free time, replans on every change and blocks the slot once time gets tight.

🔒

Encrypted towards Google

Titles, places and notes are encrypted on your device. Google keeps only an unreadable record and shows your colleagues a plain “Blocked”.

⇄

Private and work

Two Google accounts, one plan. A doctor's appointment keeps your work calendar free without saying why.

📂

A folder, not a silo

Every task is an iCalendar file. Scripts and AI agents may help out – every change waits for you in Review first.

✦

Local assistant

“The report by Friday, about four hours” – the built-in assistant runs on Ollama on your computer and only makes proposals.

⟳

Several devices, even offline

Laptop and phone may change things at the same time. Kaboome merges conflicts field by field instead of overwriting anything.

How it works

No server of ours, no account with us. Google Calendar is just the transport.

  1. Connect

    Kaboome signs in to your Google accounts directly. Your key is created on the device and stays there.

  2. Add tasks

    What needs doing, how long it takes, by when. Sleep, breaks or the end of the workday are simply recurring events.

  3. Kaboome plans

    Blocks move into free time and step aside when someone books a meeting there. A click on “Blocked” takes you back into the app.

What Google sees – and what it doesn't

Google needs to know when you are busy. Nothing more.

  • Google sees: when you are busy or free – merged, without linking it to individual tasks.
  • Google does not see: titles, places, notes, priorities, deadlines and recurrence of your own entries.
  • Encryption: AES-256-GCM, key derivation with HKDF-SHA-512, following the algorithm choices of CNSA 2.0 and BSI TR-02102.
  • Keys: only on your devices; new devices are paired with a recovery code.
  • This website: no cookies, no trackers, no fonts or scripts from third parties.
  • Links back into the app: the reference sits after the “#” and never leaves your browser.

Kaboome is an experimental project and not certified. Events other people invite you to remain in plain text at Google – that is where they come from.
